Upset Fish Privacy Policy

Developer: Freezing Point
Effective September 21, 2026

This policy describes how Freezing Point handles information in Upset Fish for Android.

Information stored by the game

Upset Fish stores level progress, earned items, preferences, and fishbowl care progress locally on your device. This version has no advertising, analytics, account login, or real-money purchase SDKs and requests no Internet permission. The game does not send personal information or gameplay data to Freezing Point, and Freezing Point does not sell game data.

Android services

Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device and account settings, Android may back up or transfer local app data. These services are controlled by Android and your backup provider. The game also includes integration with your device's text-to-speech service; that provider's own privacy practices apply to its operation.

Storage and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear it or remove the app, subject to Android backup and restore settings. To delete local progress, open Android Settings > Apps > Upset Fish > Storage > Clear data. This resets your progress and preferences. Manage backups separately through your device or backup provider.

Support messages

If you email Freezing Point, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to answer your request. No support messages are sent automatically by the game. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask players for personal information, and Freezing Point does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information in a support message, contact us to request its deletion.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when you visit, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. This page includes no analytics scripts or advertising.

Changes and contact

Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Freezing Point at 4incode@protonmail.com.
